# Finance Solutions on the TACEO Network

Public blockchains expose everything by default: balances, counterparties, amounts, transaction flows. That's a dealbreaker for real financial operations. TACEO's finance solutions let developers, institutions, and AI agents move value on the chains they already use without putting their business on a public ledger.

## Merces — private onchain finance

Our finance offering is one privacy solution, **Merces**, with multiple features on top. Merces is TACEO's confidential token transfer protocol. It wraps existing ERC-20 tokens into a private form. Balances are held as secret shares across the TACEO Network, state transitions are verified onchain via coSNARKs, and no single party — including TACEO — sees what anyone holds or sends. Compliance primitives are built into the protocol, not bolted on top.

:::tip What is Merces?
Merces is an integrable privacy layer for the chain you already use. Two things set it apart:

- **We come to you.** Merces deploys on the chain you already use. No migration, no new asset.
- **Solutions, not primitives.** A ready-made solution you can plug into a stablecoin or fintech product, not raw cryptographic building blocks you have to assemble.

Canonical write-up: [core.taceo.io/articles/merces-onchain-finance](https://core.taceo.io/articles/merces-onchain-finance/).
:::

For what is live today — chains, frontends, feature availability — see [Deployments & status](/docs/start-here/deployments-and-status).

## Features

### Private virtual account

An encrypted balance on the chain the user is already on, standalone or alongside their public one. Deposit, transfer, withdraw, and read your own history — without publishing any of it.

**Best fit:**

- Payroll, treasury, and B2B settlement that can't be public
- Stablecoin issuers and fintechs offering confidential accounts
- White-labeled privacy rails inside an existing product

[Learn more →](/docs/finance-solutions/private-virtual-account/overview)

</div>

<div className="service-card-primary">

### Private DeFi

Put a private balance to work without exposing positions, counterparties, or strategy. Yield today, with swaps and further composability in development.

**Best fit:**

- Treasuries earning on balances they can't disclose
- Traders and LPs who would otherwise broadcast strategy
- Regulated deployments that need privacy and audit together

[Learn more →](/docs/finance-solutions/yield/introduction)

</div>

<div className="service-card-primary">

### Compliance

Sanctions and AML screening before a transfer settles, and scoped disclosure of past activity to authorised parties afterwards — through MPC, not surveillance infrastructure.

**Best fit:**

- VASPs and regulated entities on private payment rails
- Fintechs integrating Merces who need compliance in the flow
- Auditors and compliance teams needing scoped reveal

[Learn more →](/docs/finance-solutions/compliance/introduction)

</div>

</div>
