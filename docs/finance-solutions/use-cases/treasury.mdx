# Treasury management

Onchain, a treasury desk's every buy, sell and rebalance is public in real time — an open invitation to front-running and MEV, and a live feed of your reserves and strategy to anyone who cares to watch.

## What Merces adds

**Confidential asset purchase and sale.** Buy and sell stablecoins and tokenized assets from a private account, with size and counterparties kept off the public ledger.

**Idle balances put to work privately.** Deposit into a vault from the private balance, with the position and its value encrypted throughout. See [Private DeFi](/docs/finance-solutions/private-defi/overview) for what is available today.

**Private rebalancing.** Allocations execute privately and only their correctness is proven onchain — private execution, public verification.

**Disclosure for auditors.** Past activity can be decrypted for authorised parties, scoped to what was asked for. See [Post-transaction disclosure](/docs/finance-solutions/compliance/post-transaction-disclosure).

**No migration.** Runs on the chain the treasury already sits on.
